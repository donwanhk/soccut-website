SocCut

A macOS editor for futsal and football match footage.

  • Import and order the clips from a match
  • Mark goals, breaks, freezes and slow-motion replays
  • A burned-in scoreboard with team colours, match title and half labels
  • Animated goal flashes in the scoring team's colour
  • Per-clip audio auto-tune, so clips recorded at different distances match

Everything happens on your Mac. Privacy policy.
